### 3️⃣ Property Listing Forms ✅
**Report:** [Form best practices guide](bc-7aecbf24-6266-5722-afbe-c6b1326e0fff)

**RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE:** 5-step wizard (Airbnb-inspired)

1. **Basic Info** - Type, Price, Location, Beds/Baths
2. **Property Details** - Area, Furnishing, Parking, Amenities
3. **Description** - Title, long-form text
4. **Photos & Media** - Drag-drop, 2-20 photos, reordering
5. **Review & Publish** - Preview, contact verification

**MUST-HAVE FIELDS (Philippine Condos):**
- Listing type, Price, Building name
- Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Floor area (sqm)
- Furnishing status, Parking slots
- Location (City, Barangay, GPS)
- Amenities checkboxes
- Photos (min 2, recommended 8-15)

- Flowloopa: ₱4,000/mo, AI messaging
- International: HubSpot (₱1,220/user), Pipedrive (₱850), Salesforce (₱1,525) - LOW adoption

**Property Management (5+ new 2025-2026):**
- ArmaRenta, APTRNT, RentFlow PH, MyRentals, BahaySuite

---

**#1 MARKET GAP (HIGHEST PRIORITY):**

**"Mobile-First CRM with Messenger/Viber Integration"**
- Market size: 10,000+ solo agents, 2,000+ small brokerages (Metro Manila)
- Current solution: Excel + WhatsApp (chaotic)
- Price point: ₱299-499/agent/mo
- TAM: ₱300M+ annual revenue

**WHY INTERNATIONAL TOOLS FAIL IN PHILIPPINES:**
❌ Email-first (PH leads arrive on Messenger/Viber, not email)  
❌ USD pricing (₱850-1,770/agent/mo doesn't scale)  
❌ No Taglish AI (Filipino customers mix languages)  
❌ No GCash/Maya payment tracking  
❌ No commission split calculator  
❌ Desktop-first (agents work in the field)  

**Result:** Agents try for 3 days → revert to Excel + WhatsApp
