# Philippine Real Estate Technology Competitive Landscape Analysis
## Research Report - November 2026

---

## Executive Summary

The Philippine PropTech market is fragmented and underserved, with clear gaps between what exists and what real estate agents actually need. While listing platforms dominate (Lamudi, PropertyGuru, Carousell), the CRM and workflow automation segment remains immature, creating significant opportunities for tools that address local payment methods, messaging channels, and commission tracking.

**Key Findings:**
- **Listing Platforms are Saturated**: Lamudi, PropertyGuru, Carousell, and Facebook Marketplace own property discovery
- **CRM Adoption is Low**: Agents rely on Excel, WhatsApp groups, and spreadsheets; international CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive) have poor local adoption due to USD pricing and lack of PH-specific integrations
- **Local CRM Tools are Emerging**: iRealtee, iGotSolutions, Flowloopa are new (2025-2026) but still small
- **Major Pain Points**: Lead fragmentation across Facebook/Viber/Messenger, manual commission tracking, no shared pipeline visibility, missed follow-ups
- **Underserved Markets**: Affordable housing (₱600K-₱1.5M), OFW buyers, landlords managing 2-20 units, small brokerages (5-20 agents)

## 3. PROPTECH ADOPTION CASE STUDIES

### Philippines Success Stories

**1. Verve Residences + Condo Control (Sept 2025)**
- **Challenge**: 1,017-unit condo; manual service requests, paper-heavy, poor OFW owner communication
- **Solution**: Condo Control property management software
- **Results**:
  - 45% resident adoption (458+ users)
  - 30-40% faster maintenance resolution
  - 50-60% reduction in phone/walk-in inquiries
  - 2-3 hours/day staff time saved
  - 50%+ reduction in printing/paper costs
  - 11-25% estimated operational cost reduction

**2. First Oceanic Property Management (FOPM) + iFAE (2022-2024)**
- **Challenge**: Slow billing, manual collections, bank deposit inefficiency
- **Solution**: iFAE (Integrated FOPM Automated Experience) - fintech-integrated property management
- **Results**:
  - Billing time: 5-6 days → 1.5 days
  - Manual invoicing/collection: 14,000 hrs/mo → 3,920 hrs/mo (10,080 hrs saved)
  - Online collections: +53% (₱951.6M → ₱1.461B)
  - Banking trips eliminated: 8,700 hrs/mo saved
  - Sustainability: 470M sheets of paper saved annually (58,700 trees)

**3. ARTHALAND + OutSystems Low-Code (April 2025)**
- **Challenge**: Isolated ERP and CRM systems; slow development; manual broker processes
- **Solution**: OutSystems low-code platform → NAVIS BY ARTHALAND suite
- **Results**:
  - Development time: Reduced to 3 months per application
  - Unified Sales, Customer, Broker, Rewards, and Handover apps
  - Real-time computation sheets for multiple payment options
  - Digital broker accreditation (eliminated paperwork delays)
  - Cloud-based workflows reduced energy consumption

**4. Lhoopa PropTech (2025)**
- **Challenge**: Affordable housing crisis; 70% of Filipinos can only afford homes <₱1.5M, but only 14% of supply is in this range
- **Solution**: AI-enabled land assessment, supply-demand matching, decentralized contractor/broker network, mobile apps
- **Results**:
  - 2.5× growth in homes sold (H1 2025 vs H1 2024)
  - Nearly 7,000 low-to-middle-income buyers served across 64 cities
  - 88% first-time buyers
  - 51% women buyers
  - Average buyer income: ₱20,000/mo; average home price: ₱650,000

**5. Noneaway Property Platform (Sept 2025)**
- **Challenge**: Duplicate listings, unverified sellers, unclear ownership
- **Solution**: Verified listings platform with anti-duplicate tech, QR sharing, anti-screenshot protection
- **Results**:
  - 850+ verified properties worth ₱60B+ onboarded
  - 300+ active brokers/agents
  - ₱8B in exclusive off-market listings
  - Expansion into 30 urban and rural markets

**6. RE/MAX Philippines + Property Raptor (2024-2025)**
- **Challenge**: Fragmented offices, disconnected tools, email/messaging for listings, manual data entry, lost deals to competitors
- **Solution**: Property Raptor CRM (international tool, customized for PH)
- **Results**:
  - Centralized listing database across franchises
  - Real-time listing sharing (eliminated multi-day email waits)
  - Prevented lost deals to competitors when listings were already in network
  - Unified contacts, documents, listing data in one place

## 4. PHILIPPINE REAL ESTATE AGENT PAIN POINTS & CRM BARRIERS

### Top Pain Points (Research-Validated)

1. **Lead Fragmentation**
   - Inquiries scattered across Facebook, Lamudi, PropertyGuru, Carousell, walk-ins, referrals, Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp
   - No centralized lead capture
   - Leads fall through cracks worth millions (RE/MAX case study)

2. **Missed Follow-Ups**
   - No systematic follow-up system
   - Agents rely on personal notes, memory, or Viber reminders
   - Hot leads go cold after 24-48 hours

3. **Manual Commission Tracking**
   - Brokers and agents track commissions in personal spreadsheets
   - Disputes over splits
   - Delayed payouts
   - No visibility into pipeline value
   - Commission genealogy (overrides, splits) not tracked

4. **Document Chaos**
   - Contracts, titles, tax declarations, permits in filing cabinets and random folders
   - Finding a document takes hours
   - No central repository

5. **No Shared Pipeline Visibility**
   - Brokerage managers can't see team performance without asking for manual reports
   - No real-time inventory or deal status
   - Agents keep separate lead lists

6. **Inefficient Property Management**
   - 45% of realtors face challenges in property information management
   - Inefficient handling of sales information (24% of challenges)
   - Difficulty managing lease agreements (24%)
   - Limited centralization of property information (20%)
   - Issues tracking listings and rental information (16%)

### Why CRM Adoption Fails in PH

1. **Generic Tools Don't Fit PH Workflows**
   - International CRMs assume leads arrive via web forms and close over email
   - PH reality: leads start with Facebook comment → move to Messenger → then Viber → then ocular visit scheduling
   - Manual logging kills adoption (agents stop after day 3)

2. **Missing Local Channel Integrations**
   - No Facebook Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp native integrations
   - GCash, Maya, bank payment tracking not supported
   - No Taglish conversational AI (Filipino customers mix Tagalog + English in one sentence)

3. **USD Pricing Doesn't Scale for Peso-Denominated Deals**
   - Salesforce $260/user/mo = ₱11,000/mo (near entry-level sales salary)
   - HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho start at $14-25/user/mo = ₱850-1,500/user/mo
   - For a 10-agent team: $140-250/mo = ₱8,500-15,250/mo
   - Economics don't work when agents earn ₱15,000-30,000/mo base

4. **Not Mobile-First**
   - PH agents work in the field (viewings, site visits, ocular inspections)
   - Need mobile-first data entry and updates
   - Desktop-first CRMs lose agent engagement

5. **Training & Onboarding Friction**
   - Complex CRMs require significant training investment
   - High agent turnover in PH (some brokerages have constant churn)
   - ROI doesn't justify training cost if agents leave after 6-12 months

6. **No Commission Split Features**
   - Critical for PH brokerage model (broker splits, overrides, agent splits)
   - Generic CRMs have no built-in commission tracking
   - Forces agents back to Excel

7. **Staff Resistance & Weak Leadership Support**
   - Agents undervalue their time (prefer manual Excel tracking)
   - Cost of leads underappreciated (no measurement of cost per lead, cost per sale)
   - Older generation of brokers/managers not tech-savvy

## 5. MARKET GAPS & DIFFERENTIATION OPPORTUNITIES

### GAP 1: Mobile-First, Messenger/Viber-Integrated CRM for Small Brokerages (5-20 agents)

**Market Need:**
- Small brokerages and teams lack affordable, PH-specific CRM
- International tools too expensive and don't fit PH channels
- New local tools (iRealtee, Flowloopa) still emerging with limited features

**Underserved Segment:**
- Solo agents and small brokerages (5-20 agents)
- Agents handling 10-50 active leads at any time
- Primarily residential sales (condos, house & lot, townhouses)
- Budget: ₱2,000-5,000/mo per agent or ₱10,000-30,000/mo for team license

**Differentiation Opportunity:**
- **Facebook Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp native integrations** (not email-first)
- **Taglish conversational AI** for instant inquiry responses
- **Commission split calculator** built-in (not a spreadsheet)
- **Shared pipeline visibility** so managers see team performance live
- **GCash/Maya payment tracking** for reservation deposits
- **Mobile-first design** with offline capability
- **Affordable pricing**: ₱199-499/agent/mo or ₱3,000-8,000/mo flat for 10-20 agent teams

**Direct Competitors to Beat:**
- Flowloopa (₱4,000/mo) — match/beat on price, exceed on features
- Excel + WhatsApp + Spreadsheets (free but chaotic) — provide 10× better organization for <₱5,000/mo

### GAP 2: Affordable Housing (₱600K-₱1.5M) End-to-End Platform

**Market Need:**
- 70% of Filipinos can only afford homes <₱1.5M, but only 14% of supply is in this range
- 75% of buyers have no knowledge of affordable housing pathways (Lhoopa data)
- Pag-IBIG has 17M active members vs 48M workforce (inclusion gap)

**Underserved Segment:**
- Service-sector workers, informal workforce, first-time buyers
- Average income: ₱15,000-25,000/mo
- Target home price: ₱650,000-₱1.5M
- Markets outside Metro Manila (Cavite, Laguna, Bulacan, Pampanga, Cebu, Davao)

**Differentiation Opportunity:**
- **AI-driven land assessment and supply-demand matching** (Lhoopa model)
- **Pag-IBIG and private lender financing navigation** (simplified application process)
- **Mobile-first buyer journey** (no desktop required)
- **Educational content** on housing pathways (financing, eligibility, process)
- **Localized delivery networks** (partner brokers, contractors in 2nd-3rd tier cities)
- **Land-to-move-in workflow** (land acquisition → development → financing → handover)

**Direct Competitors:**
- Lhoopa (already executing this model, growing 2.5× YoY)
- Traditional developers (Camella, Lumina, Amaia) — slower, less tech-enabled

### GAP 3: OFW Remote Property Buying & Investment Platform

**Market Need:**
- 81,400 unsold condo units in Metro Manila
- Millions of OFWs/overseas Filipinos financially capable but underserved
- Remote buying process complex (can't view properties, trust issues, financing barriers)

**Underserved Segment:**
- OFWs in Middle East, US, Canada, Singapore, Hong Kong
- Seafarers (high earning, stable income, strong demand)
- Target properties: ₱2M-6M condos in Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao

**Differentiation Opportunity:**
- **Virtual property tours and verification** (not just 360 photos — verified authenticity)
- **WhatsApp-first communication** (OFWs prefer WhatsApp over Viber/Messenger)
- **Remote financing and documentation** (e-signatures, digital contract signing)
- **Escrow and trust mechanisms** (third-party verification of property ownership, title)
- **AI smart property matching** based on remittance patterns, lifestyle, investment goals
- **Developer quota transparency** (show foreign ownership quota status for condos)

**Direct Competitors:**
- Bahai Deals (RCBC + Talino Venture Studios) — new entrant, AI-focused
- Traditional brokers (slow, manual, trust-based)
- Lamudi/PropertyGuru (listings only, no end-to-end service)

### GAP 4: Landlord Property Management for 2-20 Units (Missing Middle)

**Market Need:**
- Small landlords (2-20 units) lack professional tools
- Larger landlords use expensive enterprise property management software
- Excel/spreadsheets still dominant for rent tracking, maintenance, financials

**Underserved Segment:**
- Landlords with 2-20 units (apartments, boarding houses, condos for rent)
- OFW landlords managing properties remotely
- First-time landlords (inherited property, investment diversification)

**Differentiation Opportunity:**
- **GCash/Maya/QR Ph automated rent collection** (not manual screenshot uploads)
- **Tenant self-service portal** (no app download, browser-based)
- **Utility sub-metering and automated splitting** (Meralco, Maynilad per unit)
- **BIR-compliant financial reports** (OR numbers, TIN tracking, income reports)
- **Maintenance ticketing with vendor dispatch** (assign trusted neighborhood repairmen)
- **Free for 2-5 units, paid from ₱149-499/mo** for 6-20 units

**Direct Competitors:**
- ArmaRenta, APTRNT, RentFlow PH, MyRentals, BahaySuite (all new, 2025-2026 entrants)
- Excel/WhatsApp/Google Sheets (free but chaotic)
- Enterprise tools (Condo Control, iFAE) — too expensive for small landlords

**Opportunity**: Consolidate the fragmented landlord tool market (5+ new entrants in 2 years) with best-in-class features + pricing.

### GAP 5: Integrated Land, Zoning, Appraisal & Feasibility Data Platform (B2B for Developers)

**Market Need:**
- Developers lack integrated access to land values, zoning rules, project feasibility, and official appraisals
- Data scattered across local government offices (manual requests, lengthy approvals)
- "Send formal letters just to obtain basic information" (PropTech Philippines)

**Underserved Segment:**
- Small-to-mid-size developers (not SMDC/Ayala Land/Megaworld scale)
- Pre-development analysis firms, consultants, investors
- Architects and planners needing zoning and land data

**Differentiation Opportunity:**
- **Consolidated public & private datasets** (land values, BIR zonal values, zoning, LGU regulations)
- **API access to government data** (partner with DENR, LGUs, BIR, Assessor's Offices)
- **AI-powered feasibility analysis** (project viability, demand forecasting, risk assessment)
- **E-government integration** (digital requests for certificates, permits)
- **Title verification and authenticity checks** (blockchain/digital signature verification)
- **B2B SaaS pricing**: ₱10,000-50,000/mo for developers, consultants

**Direct Competitors:**
- None (this is a greenfield opportunity identified by student PropTech proposals)
- Manual government office visits (slow, fragmented)

### GAP 6: AI-Powered Lead Qualification & Auto-Scheduling for Agent Productivity

**Market Need:**
- Agents spend hours answering basic property inquiries (price, size, location, availability)
- Manual back-and-forth scheduling viewings (Viber/Messenger ping-pong)
- Leads go cold because agents can't respond 24/7 (inquiries come at 9pm-11pm)

**Differentiation Opportunity:**
- **AI chatbot answers property FAQs** from approved listing sheet (price, size, location, inclusions)
- **Auto-qualification questions** (budget range, preferred area, buying purpose, timeline)
- **Self-service viewing scheduling** (prospects pick available slots from agent's calendar)
- **Taglish conversational AI** (handles "hi po, magkano yung package nyo? available pa ba this Saturday?")
- **24/7 instant replies** (speed to first reply moves more revenue than anything else)
- **Hand-off to agent when qualified** (with full conversation history)

**Direct Competitors:**
- Flowloopa (has AI auto-messages, but limited features)
- Tara AI, AI Automation Services PH (custom builds, likely expensive)
- ChatDaddy (WhatsApp-focused, not real estate-specific)
