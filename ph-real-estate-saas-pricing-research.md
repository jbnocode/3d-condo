# Philippine Real Estate Agent SaaS - Pricing Research & Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document provides comprehensive pricing research and strategic recommendations for a Philippine real estate agent SaaS tool with optional 3D scanning services. The analysis covers market demographics, competitor pricing, unit economics, payment methods, and revenue projections for both B2B (agent-only) and B2B2C (marketplace) models.

---

## 1. Philippine Real Estate Agent Market Demographics

### Commission Rates & Income

**Standard Commission Structure:**
- **Horizontal/Economic Housing**: 6-7% of sale price
- **Condominiums (Vertical)**: 3-5% of sale price
- **Pre-owned Properties**: ~5% of sale price
- **Standard Rate**: Most commonly 5% overall

**Monthly Income Distribution** (2024 Davao Survey):
- ₱20,000-50,000: 47.5% of agents
- ₱51,000-80,000: 37.5% of agents
- ₱81,000-100,000: 11.25% of agents
- Above ₱100,000: 3.75% of agents

**Per-Transaction Earnings**:
- Average: ₱12,769 - ₱26,297 per deal
- Average base salary: ₱18,840/month
- Entry-level agents: ~₱265,081 annually
- Senior agents: ~₱572,723 annually

**Key Insights:**
- Highly variable, commission-based income
- Most agents earn below ₱50,000/month
- Tool pricing must account for irregular cash flow
- Affordable monthly fees are critical (under 5% of average monthly income)

### Tech Adoption Status

**Current State (2024):**
- **Real estate software usage**: 72.7% of agents
- **CRM software adoption**: Nearly 0% (traditional agents)
- **Spreadsheets/Paper**: Still common (45.4% combined)
- **Digital marketing**: Growing but uneven
- **Virtual tours/AR**: Low adoption due to cost barriers

**Adoption Characteristics:**
- High mobile usage (field-based work model)
- Strong preference for simple, no-training-required tools
- Cost sensitivity dominates purchase decisions
- 3-6 month B2B sales cycles for property management software
- Social/referral networks drive adoption

**Barriers to Adoption:**
- Budget constraints for advanced features
- Lack of technical skills training
- Resistance to change from paper/traditional methods
- Skepticism about ROI on tech investments

## 3. Customer Acquisition & Retention Metrics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**Industry Benchmarks:**
- **PropTech B2B SaaS**: $185 (~₱10,175) per customer (2026 benchmark)
- **B2B SaaS (General)**: $239 (~₱13,145) per customer
- **Philippine SaaS Estimate**: $230-430 (~₱12,650-23,650) per customer

**Recommended Planning Benchmark**: ₱10,000-15,000 per customer
- Organic (SEO/content): Lower end (₱10,000-12,000)
- Paid (ads/referrals): Higher end (₱13,000-18,000)

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Benchmark Assumptions:**
- Average subscription: ₱1,500/month
- Average customer lifetime: 24 months (conservative)
- Gross margin: 80% (SaaS standard)
- **LTV = ₱1,500 × 24 × 0.80 = ₱28,800**

**Target Ratio**: LTV:CAC should be 3:1 minimum
- If CAC = ₱12,000, LTV must be ≥ ₱36,000
- Requires: 30-month retention OR ₱1,800/month ARPU OR expansion revenue

### Churn Rates

**SMB SaaS Benchmarks:**
- **Monthly churn**: 3-7% (36-76% annualized) for SMB
- **Annual churn target**: 10-15% (good performance)
- **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)**: Aim for 85-90%
- **Philippine SaaS estimate**: 4.8% monthly (~48% annual, modeled)

**For Philippine Real Estate Context:**
- Expect higher churn (5-7% monthly) due to:
  - Irregular agent income
  - Economic volatility
  - Low switching costs
  - Seasonal real estate cycles
- Mitigation strategies critical (see recommendations)

### Trial/Freemium Conversion

**Conversion Benchmarks:**
| Model | Visitor→Trial/Free | Trial/Free→Paid | Best For |
|-------|-------------------|-----------------|----------|
| Free Trial (No CC) | 8.5% | 18.2% | New products, trust-building |
| Free Trial (CC Required) | 2.5% | 48.8% | Established brands |
| Freemium | 13.3% | 2.6% | Network-effect products |

**Recommendation for PH Market:**
- **14-30 day free trial, no credit card required**
- Expected conversion: 15-20% trial-to-paid
- Creates urgency without friction
- Better than freemium for B2B SaaS (freemium converts at only 2-5%)

## 4. Payment Methods in the Philippines

### Popular Payment Options

**E-Wallets (Essential):**
1. **GCash** - Market leader
   - One-time payments: ✅ Supported
   - Recurring billing: ❌ Not natively supported by PayMongo/Maya
   - Workaround: Use dLocal (via Recurly) for GCash recurring

2. **Maya (PayMaya)** - Second most popular
   - One-time payments: ✅ Supported
   - Recurring billing: ✅ Supported (PayMongo, EBANX)
   - Better for subscriptions than GCash

**Cards:**
- Visa/Mastercard credit/debit cards
- 3D Secure authentication standard
- Card vaulting for recurring subscriptions
- Supported by all major gateways

**Buy Now Pay Later:**
- **BillEase** - Installment payments
  - 0-5% monthly interest
  - Up to ₱50,000 credit limit
  - Merchant gets paid upfront; BillEase carries risk
  - Good for higher-priced packages (₱5,000+/year)

**Direct Bank Transfers:**
- BPI Online, UnionBank, BDO, Metrobank, Landbank
- Supported via PayMongo, Xendit
- Manual reconciliation required

### Recommended Payment Gateway Stack

**Primary Gateway**: **PayMongo**
- Supports: Cards, Maya, GCash (one-time), bank transfers
- Recurring billing: Cards + Maya only
- ₱0 setup, ~2.9% + ₱15 per transaction
- Best for Philippine SaaS subscriptions

**For GCash Recurring**: **Recurly + dLocal**
- Enables GCash subscription enrollment
- Higher setup complexity
- Only add if GCash recurring is critical

**For Installments**: **BillEase Integration**
- For annual plans (₱10,000+)
- Merchant paid upfront; customer pays installments
- Increases affordability perception

### Installment Billing Strategy

**Use Case**: Annual subscriptions for agents with irregular income

**Implementation**:
1. Use **Mochi.ph** for installment schedule management
2. Connect to PayMongo for payment collection
3. Offer quarterly or monthly installments on annual plans

**Example**: ₱12,000 annual plan
- Monthly installments: ₱1,100/month × 12 (10% premium)
- Quarterly installments: ₱3,300/quarter × 4 (5% premium)

## 6. Unit Economics Breakdown

### Assumptions (Professional Tier, Model 1)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Monthly Subscription Revenue** | ₱1,499 |
| **COGS (hosting, support, 3rd party)** | ₱225 (15%) |
| **Gross Margin** | ₱1,274 (85%) |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | ₱12,000 |
| **Average Customer Lifetime** | 18 months (conservative) |
| **Monthly Churn Rate** | 6% |

### Calculations

**Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):**
```
LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Monthly Churn
LTV = (₱1,499 × 0.85) / 0.06
LTV = ₱21,233
```

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
```
LTV:CAC = ₱21,233 / ₱12,000 = 1.77:1
```

**⚠️ WARNING: Below 3:1 threshold!**

### Path to Profitability

**Option A: Reduce Churn to 4%**
```
LTV = (₱1,499 × 0.85) / 0.04 = ₱31,849
LTV:CAC = 2.65:1 (better but still low)
```

**Option B: Increase ARPU to ₱1,999 + Reduce Churn to 4.5%**
```
LTV = (₱1,999 × 0.85) / 0.045 = ₱37,756
LTV:CAC = 3.15:1 ✅ HEALTHY
```

**Option C: Add 3D Scanning Revenue**
```
Base LTV = ₱21,233
3D scan attachment rate: 30% of customers
Avg scans per customer lifetime: 3 scans × ₱6,999 = ₱20,997
3D gross margin: 40% (₱8,399)

Total LTV = ₱21,233 + ₱8,399 = ₱29,632
LTV:CAC = 2.47:1 (better, not yet healthy)
```

**Recommendation**: **Combine B + C**
- Base ARPU: ₱1,999/month
- Churn: 4.5% (aggressive retention efforts)
- 3D upsells: 30% attachment
- **Total LTV: ₱37,756 + ₱8,399 = ₱46,155**
- **LTV:CAC: 3.85:1 ✅ STRONG**

### Recommendation 2: Price Professional Tier at ₱1,499/month

**Justification**:
- 3-5% of average agent income (₱30,000-50,000/month)
- Competitive vs Lamudi (₱2,499) and Casa PH (₱999)
- Positions as premium but accessible
- Allows 20% discount for annual (₱14,390/year = ₱1,199/mo)
- Target ARPU aligns with unit economics model

**Pricing Psychology**:
- ₱1,499 feels significantly cheaper than ₱2,000+
- Compare to "cost of 1-2 Lamudi featured listings per month"
- Frame as "pays for itself with 1 extra lead per month"

---

### Recommendation 3: Bundle 3D Scanning Strategically

**Strategy**:
❌ **DON'T**: Buy Matterport Pro3 (₱220,000 + ₱2,750/mo) upfront
✅ **DO**: Partner with existing 3D scanning providers initially

**Phase 1 (Months 1-12)**: **White-Label Partner Model**
- Contract with Meta Venues or similar local providers
- Negotiate bulk rates: ₱5,000-6,000 per scan (vs ₱15,000+ retail)
- Mark up to ₱6,999-9,999 for customers
- Gross margin: ₱1,500-3,000 per scan
- No hardware investment required

**Phase 2 (Months 13-24)**: **Acquire Equipment** (if volume justifies)
- Once doing 50+ scans/month consistently
- Break-even analysis:
  - Equipment: ₱250,000 (camera + accessories)
  - Hosting: ₱2,750/month
  - Labor: ₱25,000/month (part-time technician)
  - Break-even: ~30-40 scans/month

**Phase 3 (Year 2+)**: **Agent Self-Service** (optional)
- DIY upload for agents with smartphones/360 cameras
- Simplified tools (not full Matterport quality)
- Price: ₱199-499 per property
- Higher margins, lower quality (acceptable trade-off)

## 11. Key Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: High Agent Churn
**Mitigation**: 
- Annual contracts with discounts
- Strong onboarding + engagement
- Prove ROI within first 30 days (track leads generated)

### Risk 2: Low 3D Scan Adoption
**Mitigation**:
- First scan free with Professional subscription
- Case studies showing 3D = faster sales
- Bulk pricing for agencies

### Risk 3: Payment Method Friction
**Mitigation**:
- Support Maya + GCash from day one
- Offer bank transfer for agencies
- Installment plans via BillEase

### Risk 4: Competition from International Players
**Mitigation**:
- Emphasize local advantage (language, payments, support)
- Price competitively (20-30% below international)
- Partner with PH brokerage associations

### Risk 5: Economic Downturn → Agent Income Drop
**Mitigation**:
- Flexible downgrade options (not just cancel)
- Pause subscriptions (up to 2 months)
- Usage-based pricing tier (pay per active listing)
