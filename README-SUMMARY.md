## ✅ What You Get: Output Comparison

### iPhone LiDAR (Polycam)
- ✅ Editable 2D/3D floor plans
- ✅ Measurements (0.5-inch accuracy)
- ✅ 3D mesh exports (.glb, .obj, .dxf)
- ✅ PDF for print
- ✅ Share links for buyers
- ⚠️ Coarse mesh (not photorealistic)
- ⚠️ 5m range limit

**Best for:** Floor plans, measurements, quick documentation

### 360 Camera (Matterport)
- ✅ Photorealistic virtual tours
- ✅ Dollhouse view (iconic feature)
- ✅ 72MP photos (X4) or 23MP (Theta Z1)
- ✅ MLS/Google integration
- ✅ AI room labels
- ⚠️ Longer capture time
- ⚠️ Higher cost

**Best for:** Premium listings, luxury condos, marketing

## ❓ Top 5 Questions Answered

### 1. "Can I use my regular iPhone?"
**No.** Need iPhone 12 Pro or later (Pro/Pro Max only have LiDAR). Standard models don't work.

### 2. "How accurate are measurements?"
**iPhone LiDAR:** Within 0.5-inch (1-10cm) - Good enough for real estate floor plans.

### 3. "Is it hard to learn?"
**No.** 2-3 practice scans (2-4 hours) and you're ready. Easier than learning Photoshop.

### 4. "Can I charge extra for 3D tours?"
**Yes.** Common: ₱5,000-10,000 premium, or bundle as value-add to commission.

### 5. "Polycam or Matterport?"
- **Polycam:** Fast, cheap, great floor plans → Use for 80% of listings
- **Matterport:** Beautiful tours, dollhouse view → Use for premium 20%
- **Best:** Use both (Hybrid approach)
