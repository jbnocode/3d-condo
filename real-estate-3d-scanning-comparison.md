# Real Estate 3D Scanning Comparison: iPhone LiDAR vs 360 Cameras
## Philippine Market Analysis & Recommendations

**Date:** November 2, 2026  
**Focus:** 2BR Condo Unit Scanning for Real Estate Listings

---

## Executive Summary

For the Philippine real estate market, **iPhone LiDAR scanning represents the most cost-effective MVP approach** for agents starting with 3D property documentation. The combination of existing iPhone ownership, free-to-affordable apps, and 5-8 minute scan times makes it ideal for DIY agent adoption. 360 cameras offer superior visual presentation quality but require higher upfront investment and longer capture times (20-35 minutes).

**Recommended MVP Path:**
1. **Start DIY:** iPhone 15 Pro (₱52,890-63,990) + Polycam Business (₱33/month or ₱400/year)
2. **Scale to Professional:** Add Insta360 X4 (₱26,390) for high-end listings requiring premium virtual tours
3. **Enterprise Option:** Matterport Pro2 (contact Norde) for agencies offering full-service 3D tours

**Key Specifications:**
- **Range:** Up to 5 meters effective distance
- **Accuracy:** 1-10 cm (varies by app, distance, and complexity)
- **Point Cloud Density:** 485-610 points/m² (iPhone) vs 2,485 points/m² (professional TLS)
- **Technology:** Time-of-flight LiDAR with VCSEL DOE (Vertical Cavity Surface Emitting Laser)

### Technical Capabilities
- **Real-time geometry capture** with SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping)
- **Automatic floor plan generation** from room scans
- **On-device processing** for most apps (no internet required for scanning)
- **Export formats:** GLB, OBJ, PLY, USDZ, DXF (varies by app)
- **Measurement accuracy:** Within 0.5-1% for room-scale captures

## 2. 3D Scanning Apps Comparison

### 2.1 Polycam - Best for Real Estate Floor Plans

**Pricing (2026):**
- **Free:** Limited features, basic scanning
- **Basic:** $30/month (₱1,680/month) - Unlimited captures, 6 mesh formats
- **Business:** $400/year per user (₱22,400/year or ₱1,867/month) - **RECOMMENDED**
- **Enterprise:** $1,200/year (3+ users)

**Business Plan Features (Best for Real Estate):**
- ✅ Editable 2D & 3D floor plans
- ✅ Automated measurements (0.5-inch tolerance)
- ✅ Point cloud exports (PLY, LAS, PTS, XYZ, DXF)
- ✅ Virtual walkthroughs
- ✅ Team collaboration & shared libraries
- ✅ PDF floor plan export with annotations
- ✅ 15+ export formats (GLTF, OBJ, FBX, DAE, STL, USDZ)

**Real Estate Workflow:**
1. **Scan:** Walk through property with iPhone (5-8 min for 2BR)
2. **Process:** On-device processing in minutes (no upload wait)
3. **Edit:** Adjust walls, add labels, measurements on any device
4. **Export:** DXF for AutoCAD, PDF for clients, GLB for web

**Pros:**
- Fast, mobile-first workflow
- Best floor plan editing tools
- Accurate measurements (0.5-inch tolerance)
- No per-space fees (unlike Matterport)
- Works offline for capture

**Cons:**
- Subscription required for floor plans
- Less polished virtual tours vs Matterport
- Requires iPhone 13+ for Space Mode

### 2.2 Matterport Capture - Best for Virtual Tours

**Pricing (2026):**
- **App:** Free download
- **Starter:** $14/month or $144/year (5 active spaces)
- **Professional:** $69/month or $696/year (20 active spaces) - **RECOMMENDED FOR AGENCIES**
- **Business:** $355/month or $3,552/year (100 active spaces)

**Professional Plan Features:**
- ✅ 20 active spaces (unlimited archiving)
- ✅ 10 users
- ✅ Hosted 3D virtual tours
- ✅ Dollhouse view
- ✅ Automated room labels & dimensions
- ✅ MLS integration (Homes.com, Apartments.com)
- ✅ Google Street View publishing
- ✅ Property Intelligence (AI descriptions)

**Add-ons (Sold Separately):**
- Schematic floor plans: $14.99 (24hrs), $19.99 (24hrs fast), $39.99 (6hrs express)
- MatterPak (E57, CAD, BIM): $59/space

**Scan Time:**
- iPhone Complete Mode: 20-45 minutes for 2,000 sq ft
- iPhone Quick Mode: 10-15 minutes

**Pros:**
- Industry-standard virtual tours
- Professional presentation quality
- Excellent for buyer engagement
- Integrated marketing tools
- MLS/Google integration

**Cons:**
- Higher ongoing cost (per-space model)
- Slower iPhone capture vs Polycam
- Requires internet for processing
- Floor plans are paid add-on

### 2.3 Canvas - Best for CAD/BIM Workflows

**Pricing:**
- **App:** Free (scan up to 2 spaces/month)

## 6. DIY Agent Scanning vs Professional Service

### 6.1 DIY Agent Approach

**Best For:**
- Individual agents
- Small agencies (2-5 agents)
- High-volume listings (20+/year)
- Budget-conscious operations
- Quick turnaround needs

**Equipment Investment:**
- **Minimum:** Own iPhone Pro model + Polycam (₱22,400/year)
- **Optimal:** iPhone + Polycam + Insta360 X4 for premium listings (₱48,790 first year)

**Time Investment:**
- **Learning:** 2-4 practice scans (2-3 hours total)
- **Per Property:** 15-30 minutes onsite + 10-20 minutes editing
- **Monthly:** 2-3 hours for 10 listings

**Revenue Potential:**
- **Value-add to listing:** Better marketing, faster sales
- **Premium fee:** ₱5,000-10,000/listing for 3D tour included
- **Break-even:** 3-5 listings (if charging premium)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Control over timeline (scan anytime)
- ✅ No per-listing costs
- ✅ Faster turnaround (same-day edits)
- ✅ Agent knows property (better annotations)
- ✅ Value-add skill for agent
- ✅ Low ongoing costs

**Cons:**
- ❌ Learning curve (2-4 properties)
- ❌ Agent time investment
- ❌ Quality depends on agent skill
- ❌ Less polished than professional
- ❌ iPhone range limitation (5m)

### 6.2 Professional Scanning Service

**Best For:**
- Luxury/high-end listings (₱20M+)
- Large properties (200+ sqm)
- Agencies outsourcing all media
- Low-volume, high-value model
- Brands requiring consistent quality

**Service Provider Options:**
1. **Matterport Philippines Partners** (TBD - check Matterport.com/providers)
2. **Local 3D Tour Services** (emerging market)
3. **Photography Studios** (adding 3D services)

**Typical Pricing (Estimated PH Market):**
- **2BR Condo (60-80 sqm):** ₱5,000-8,000
- **3BR Condo (100-150 sqm):** ₱8,000-12,000
- **House (200+ sqm):** ₱12,000-20,000+
- **Rush Fee:** +20-50%
- **Additional Services:**
  - Floor plans: +₱2,000-3,000
  - Measurement report: +₱1,500
  - Branded tour: +₱1,000-2,000

**Turnaround:**
- Standard: 3-5 business days
- Rush: 24-48 hours (+fee)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Professional quality guaranteed
- ✅ No agent time investment
- ✅ Best visual presentation
- ✅ Consistent output
- ✅ Handle large/complex properties
- ✅ No equipment to buy/maintain

**Cons:**
- ❌ Per-property costs (₱5,000-20,000)
- ❌ Scheduling dependencies
- ❌ Longer turnaround
- ❌ Less control over output
- ❌ Higher break-even point
- ❌ Recurring expense

### 6.3 Hybrid Approach (RECOMMENDED FOR AGENCIES)

**Strategy:**
- **DIY:** Standard listings (80% of inventory)
  - 1BR, 2BR, 3BR condos
  - Houses under 200 sqm
  - Quick turnaround needs
  - Budget listings (under ₱10M)

- **Professional:** Premium listings (20% of inventory)
  - Luxury condos (penthouse, high-floor)
  - Large houses (200+ sqm)
  - High-value properties (₱20M+)
  - Commercial spaces
  - Architectural showcases

**Implementation:**
1. **Train agents** on iPhone + Polycam for standard workflow
2. **Outsource** complex/premium properties to professional service
3. **Quality threshold:** If DIY scan isn't excellent, reshoot or outsource

## 8. Sample Quality & Examples

### 8.1 What to Expect from Each Method

#### iPhone LiDAR (.glb exports)
**Characteristics:**
- Coarse mesh (~500 points/m²)
- Rounded edges and corners
- Simplified geometry
- Acceptable for layout/dimensions
- NOT photorealistic

**Best Used For:**
- Floor plan generation
- Room measurements
- Virtual staging base models
- Quick property documentation
- CAD reference

**Accuracy:** 1-10cm depending on app, distance, room complexity

**Sample Scenarios:**
- **Successful:** Rectangular rooms, good lighting, 3-5m scan paths
- **Challenging:** Long hallways, mirrors, glass walls, complex layouts
- **Failed:** Dark rooms, featureless walls, >5m range

#### 360 Camera Virtual Tours
**Characteristics:**
- High-resolution panoramic photos (23MP-72MP)
- Photorealistic imagery
- Immersive dollhouse view (Matterport)
- Accurate spatial relationships
- Professional presentation

**Best Used For:**
- Virtual property tours
- Buyer engagement
- Marketing materials
- MLS listings
- Social media content

**Visual Quality:** 
- **Insta360 X4:** 72MP photos, 8K video - Excellent detail
- **Ricoh Theta Z1:** 23MP with RAW - Good low-light, professional
- **Matterport Processing:** Seamless stitching, dollhouse view

**Sample Scenarios:**
- **Successful:** Well-lit interiors, clean staging, strategic camera positions
- **Challenging:** Very bright windows + dark interiors (use HDR)
- **Failed:** Moving objects, people in frame, insufficient coverage

### 8.2 Research-Validated Accuracy Data

From peer-reviewed studies on iPhone LiDAR:

**iPhone 13 Pro LiDAR Accuracy (Multiple Apps):**
- **Small objects (<1m):** 1.3-2.0 cm RMS deviation
- **Room interiors (15-25m²):** 8.1-10.3 cm RMS deviation
- **Building facades:** 3.7-8.5 cm RMS deviation
- **With glass/mirrors:** Up to 50% increased error

**Best Performers by Application:**
- **SiteScape:** 6cm std deviation, 83% points within 5cm
- **Polycam:** 7cm std deviation, 77% points within 5cm
- **Scaniverse:** 8cm std deviation
- **3D Scanner App:** 9cm std deviation

**Key Findings:**
- SLAM drift accumulates on long paths (loop closures help)
- Glass/reflective surfaces cause 50% higher error
- Feature-poor walls (plain white) reduce tracking quality
- Point density does NOT equal accuracy (fewer points can be more accurate)

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I use a non-Pro iPhone?
**A:** No. Only iPhone 12 Pro, 13 Pro, 14 Pro, 15 Pro, 16 Pro (and Max variants) have LiDAR. Standard models (iPhone 12, 13, 14, 15, 16) do not. You can use photogrammetry (photo-based) on any iPhone, but it's slower and less accurate for floor plans.

### Q2: How accurate are the measurements?
**A:** 
- **Polycam iPhone:** Within 0.5-inch (1.27cm) for standard interiors, 1-10cm typical
- **Matterport iPhone:** ~1% accuracy for room dimensions
- **Matterport Pro2:** <1% (within 1cm for most measurements)
- **360 cameras alone:** Not accurate (photo only); requires platform processing

*Accuracy decreases with: distance >5m, glass/mirrors, poor lighting, fast scanning*

### Q3: Do I need internet to scan?
**A:**
- **Polycam:** No internet needed for scanning or floor plan (processes on-device)
- **Matterport:** Internet required for processing (uploads to cloud)
- **Canvas:** Internet required for CAD conversion (pay service)

*Recommendation: Polycam for flexibility, especially in buildings with poor signal*

### Q4: How long does scanning really take?
**A: 2BR Condo (60-70 sqm):**
- **iPhone LiDAR (Polycam):** 5-8 min scan + 2-5 min processing + 10-20 min editing = **20-35 min total**
- **iPhone + Matterport:** 20-30 min scan + 30-60 min cloud processing + 15 min editing = **65-105 min total**
- **360 camera:** 20-30 min shoot + 30-60 min processing + 15 min editing = **65-105 min total**

*First-time users: add 50-100% to these times*

### Q5: What if the property is bigger than 5 meters?
**A:** iPhone LiDAR range is ~5m, but:
- **Walk in overlapping paths:** Scan from multiple positions
- **SLAM stitches together:** App combines multiple scans
- **Large properties:** Takes longer, may need loop closures
- **Very large (200+ sqm):** Consider 360 camera or professional service

### Q6: Can I charge extra for 3D tours?
**A:** Yes, common approaches:
- **Premium listing package:** ₱5,000-10,000 extra for 3D tour included
- **Bundled value-add:** Include free (absorb cost in commission)
- **Tiered pricing:** Basic photos vs Premium (3D + photos)

*Market positioning: "Virtual tours attract 67% more views" (justify premium)*

### Q7: Will buyers in the Philippines use 3D tours?
**A:** Yes, growing adoption:
- **Metro Manila condos:** High demand, especially for OFWs/expats
- **Preselling projects:** Buyers want to "see" before it's built
- **COVID legacy:** Remote viewing normalized
- **Mobile-friendly:** Most tours work on phones (how PH buyers browse)

*Start educating market: add tutorial/instructions in listing*

### Q8: Polycam vs Matterport - which is better?
**A:** Depends on use case:

**Choose Polycam if:**
- High-volume agent (many listings)
- Need fast turnaround (same-day)
- Want editable floor plans included
- Prefer DIY control
- Budget-conscious (no per-space fee)

**Choose Matterport if:**
- Focus on premium presentation
- Want dollhouse view (iconic feature)
- Need MLS/Google integration
- Lower volume (5-20 listings/month)
- Willing to pay per-space

**Best: Use both** - Polycam for standard, Matterport for premium

### Q9: Should I buy Insta360 X3 or X4?
**A:** 
**Insta360 X4** (₱26,390) is better value:
- 72MP photos vs 18MP (X3) - **4x resolution**
- 8K video vs 5.7K - better quality
- Active HDR - handles bright windows better
- Longer battery (135 min vs 80 min)

**Get X3 (₱16,490) only if:**
