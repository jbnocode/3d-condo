## 🎯 CRITICAL DIFFERENTIATORS (Your Competitive Advantage)

### 1. **Messaging-Native (Not Email-First)**
- ✅ Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp unified inbox
- ✅ Taglish conversational AI (not English-only bots)
- ✅ 24/7 auto-responses in Filipino customer language
- ❌ **Competitors**: All international CRMs are email-first; local tools have limited messaging AI

### 2. **Commission Tracking Built-In**
- ✅ Commission split calculator (broker/agent percentages)
- ✅ Commission genealogy tree (overrides, multi-level splits)
- ✅ Automated commission reports per agent/project
- ❌ **Competitors**: Zero international CRMs have this; only iRealtee claims it (unproven)

### 3. **GCash/Maya Payment Verification**
- ✅ Auto-verify payment screenshots (detect fake receipts)
- ✅ Track reservation deposits by payment channel
- ❌ **Competitors**: Nobody has this (all manual screenshot uploads)

### 4. **Mobile-First with Offline Mode**
- ✅ Agents can log leads/update pipeline offline (field work)
- ✅ Syncs when back online
- ❌ **Competitors**: All are online-only (agents lose data in poor connectivity areas)

### 5. **Affordable Peso Pricing**
- ✅ ₱299-499/agent/mo (or ₱3,999/mo flat for 10 agents)
- ✅ No USD exchange rate risk
- ❌ **Competitors**: International tools ₱850-1,770/agent/mo; Flowloopa ₱4,000/mo

### 6. **Lead Portal Integration**
- ✅ Direct API integration with Lamudi, PropertyGuru, Carousell
- ✅ Auto-capture leads from Facebook Lead Ads
- ❌ **Competitors**: Manual CSV uploads or expensive Zapier integrations

### 7. **< 1 Hour Setup Time**
- ✅ Sign up → Connect Messenger → Import listings → Start capturing leads in < 1 hour
- ❌ **Competitors**: HubSpot (1-2 weeks), Salesforce (4-8 weeks), even local tools need 1-2 days

## 🚀 FEATURE ROADMAP (MVP → Scale)

### MVP (Month 1-3): Core Agent Workflow
**Must-Have Features:**
- ✅ Messenger/Viber unified inbox
- ✅ Basic Taglish AI auto-responses ("hi po, magkano?")
- ✅ 5-stage pipeline (New → Contacted → Viewing → Offer → Closed)
- ✅ Commission split calculator (2-level: broker/agent)
- ✅ Mobile-responsive web app
- ✅ Google Calendar sync for viewing scheduler
- ✅ Basic agent dashboard (leads, viewings, commissions)

**Skip for MVP:**
- ❌ WhatsApp integration (start Messenger/Viber only)
- ❌ GCash/Maya auto-verification (manual upload first)
- ❌ Offline mode (online-only for MVP)
- ❌ Portal API integrations (manual lead import first)

---

### V2 (Month 4-6): Automation & Integrations
**Add Features:**
- ✅ WhatsApp Business API integration
- ✅ 24/7 AI inquiry responses (advanced Taglish)
- ✅ Auto-qualification questions (budget, location, timeline)
- ✅ Lead routing rules (assign by project/location)
- ✅ GCash/Maya payment screenshot verification
- ✅ Lamudi/PropertyGuru API lead import
- ✅ Facebook Lead Ads direct integration
- ✅ Email/SMS reminders for viewings

---

### V3 (Month 7-12): Team & Brokerage Features
**Add Features:**
- ✅ Team dashboard (manager view of all agents)
- ✅ Agent performance reports (conversion rates, response times)
- ✅ Commission genealogy (3+ level splits, overrides)
- ✅ Listing inventory management (project-based, unit status)
- ✅ Offline mode for mobile agents
- ✅ Advanced reporting (lead source ROI, funnel analytics)
- ✅ Agent leaderboards & gamification

---

### V4 (Year 2): Scale & Adjacent Markets
**Add Features:**
- ✅ Landlord property management module (rent collection, tenant portals)
- ✅ Developer portal (unit inventory, sales tracking, agent performance)
- ✅ White-label options for large brokerages
- ✅ API for third-party integrations (accounting, BIR filing)
- ✅ AI predictive lead scoring (which leads will close)
