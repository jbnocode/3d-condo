## Key Strategic Decisions

### ✅ DO THIS

1. **Start with Freemium + Paid Tiers** (not paid-only or transaction-based)
   - Why: Lowest entry barrier, proven 18% conversion, network effects
   
2. **Price Professional at ₱1,499/month**
   - Why: 3-5% of agent income, competitive vs Lamudi/Casa, healthy margins
   
3. **Partner with 3D Scanning Provider** (don't buy equipment yet)
   - Why: No ₱220k+ upfront investment, test demand first
   
4. **Use PayMongo Only** (launch)
   - Why: Covers GCash, Maya, cards; 90% of market; simple integration
   
5. **Offer 21-Day Free Trial** (no credit card)
   - Why: 18% conversion, builds trust, removes friction

### ❌ DON'T DO THIS

1. **Don't launch paid-only** (no free tier)
   - Why: Higher CAC, slower growth, agents want to test first
   
2. **Don't price below ₱999/month**
   - Why: Unsustainable unit economics, perceived as low quality
   
3. **Don't buy Matterport Pro3 camera immediately**
   - Why: ₱220k investment + ₱2,750/mo; payback needs 50+ scans/month
   
4. **Don't launch marketplace model first**
   - Why: Complex, longer sales cycle, higher capital requirement
   
5. **Don't require credit card for free trial**
   - Why: 70% lower signup rate, bad for PH market trust
