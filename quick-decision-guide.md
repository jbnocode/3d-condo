## iPhone LiDAR: The MVP

### What You Get
- ✅ **Floor plans** with measurements (0.5-inch accuracy)
- ✅ **3D models** (.glb exports for web)
- ✅ **Fast workflow** (5-8 min scan, on-device processing)
- ✅ **Unlimited scans** (no per-listing fees)
- ✅ **Easy to learn** (2-3 practice scans)

### What You Need
- **iPhone 12 Pro or later** (₱52,890+ if buying, ₱0 if owned)
- **Polycam Business** (₱22,400/year or ₱1,867/month)
- That's it. No tripod, no accessories.

### The Numbers (50 listings/year)
- **Cost per listing:** ₱448
- **vs Outsourcing:** Save ₱295,000/year
- **Break-even:** 3-5 listings

### Workflow
1. Walk through property with iPhone (6 minutes)
2. App processes on device (3 minutes)
3. Edit floor plan, add labels (15 minutes)
4. Export PDF, share link
5. **Total: 25 minutes**

### Where It Falls Short
- ❌ Not as visually impressive as 360 tours
- ❌ 5-meter range limit (need overlapping scans for large spaces)
- ❌ Coarse mesh (not photorealistic)

**Bottom Line:** Perfect for standard 1BR-3BR condos under ₱10M. Gets you 80% of the value for 5% of the cost.

## 360 Camera: The Premium Option

### What You Get
- ✅ **Virtual tours** with dollhouse view (Matterport)
- ✅ **Photorealistic** 72MP images (Insta360 X4)
- ✅ **Professional presentation** for luxury listings
- ✅ **Buyer engagement** (67% more views)

### What You Need
- **Insta360 X4:** ₱26,390 (best value, 72MP, 8K video)
- **OR Ricoh Theta Z1:** ₱39,990 (better low-light)
- **Tripod:** ₱3,000-5,000
- **Matterport Professional:** ₱58,464/year (20 active spaces)
- **Total Year 1:** ₱87,854 (X4) or ₱101,454 (Theta)

### The Numbers (50 listings/year)
- **Cost per listing:** ₱1,757
- **vs iPhone:** ₱65,454 more expensive
- **ROI:** If premium pricing adds ₱2,000+/listing

### Workflow
1. Set up tripod, capture 15-25 positions (25 minutes)
2. Upload to Matterport cloud (30-60 minutes automated)
3. Add annotations, labels, branding (20 minutes)
4. **Total: 75-105 minutes**

### Where to Use It
- ✅ Luxury condos (₱15M+)
- ✅ Houses >150 sqm
- ✅ Preselling projects (visualize unbuilt units)
- ✅ Expat/OFW buyers (remote viewing)
- ✅ High-competition market (differentiation)

**Bottom Line:** Worth it for top 20% of inventory. Overkill for standard listings.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I use a regular iPhone?**  
A: No. Need 12 Pro or later (Pro models only have LiDAR).

**Q: Is it hard to learn?**  
A: No. 2-3 practice scans (2-4 hours) and you're proficient.

**Q: How accurate are measurements?**  
A: iPhone: 0.5-1% (within 1-10cm). Good enough for real estate floor plans.

**Q: Do I need internet?**  
A: Polycam: No (processes on-device). Matterport: Yes (cloud processing).

**Q: What if property is bigger than 5 meters?**  
A: Walk in overlapping paths. App stitches together. Large properties (200+ sqm) are slower but work.

**Q: Can I charge extra for 3D tours?**  
A: Yes. Common: ₱5,000-10,000 premium, or include free as value-add.

**Q: Will Filipino buyers use 3D tours?**  
A: Yes. High demand in Metro Manila, especially OFWs/expats. Growing fast.

**Q: Polycam vs Matterport?**  
A: Polycam = fast + cheap, good floor plans. Matterport = polished tours + dollhouse. Use both (Polycam for standard, Matterport for premium).

**Q: Should I get Insta360 X3 or X4?**  
A: X4. Worth the ₱9,900 extra (72MP vs 18MP, 8K vs 5.7K, better HDR, longer battery).

**Q: Where do I start?**  
A: 
1. Subscribe Polycam Business (7-day trial)
2. Scan your home 2-3 times (practice)
3. Scan your next listing
4. Repeat, refine, scale
